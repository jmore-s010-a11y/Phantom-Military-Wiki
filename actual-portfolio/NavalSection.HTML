NAVAL SECTION (Both water and space)

You're probably wondering "water? What?" Well it's because we used to have water naval ships, and you're probably thinking of those wooden ships, no, ours were metal. Despite it being the 2200s, we decided to build them still. They stayed for a while, but finally they were retired. We had missile cruisers, nuclear submarines, no, not only they could fire nuclear warheads, they ran on it too. We also had battleships, which were more outdated, destroyers, and aircraft carriers. But now we have the space versions of them, and now have frigates and corvettes.
